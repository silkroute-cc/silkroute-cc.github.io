（吉隆坡24日讯）敬业（Kinergy Advancement Bhd，KL:KAB）与吉打州投资公司（Permodalan Kedah Bhd）签署谅解备忘录（MOU），以发展可持续能源项目。   根据文告，敬业将承担技术责任，并利用在能源领域的经验，而吉打州投资公司将为可再生能源项目寻找潜在土地，并协助履行获得项目许可等授权职责。   敬业表示，此次合作的重点是部署清洁能源、可再生能源、节能解决方案项目和工程等。这包括开发太阳能发电场和废物转化能源。   敬业执行副主席拿督黎敬安说：“这份谅解备忘录还提出了加速发展的愿景，旨在将吉打州打造为进步和创新的灯塔。”   “敬业重视吉打州投资公司致力于通过创新技术推动吉打州的发展，这些技术优先考虑减少环境影响，同时实现能源节约、稳定和弹性，符合吉打州的可持续发展目标。”   上述谅解备忘录于6月19日生效，有效期为2年。   敬业估计，上述谅解备忘录将不会显著影响截至今年12月杪财年（2024财年）的收益，但有望为未来收益作出积极贡献。   黎敬安是敬业的最大股东，直接持股权达19.54%，并通过Stocqtech私人有限公司间接持股9.23%。   其他大股东包括Tong Kum Loong，持股权为9.03%，并通过Regalis Investment Ltd及Gemini Bliss私人有限公司间接持股1.59%。   截至今年3月杪首季（2024财年首季），该集团的净利翻倍至497万令吉，一年前为243万令吉，得益于可持续能源解决方案业务提高贡献。   营业额从4276万令吉，按年微跌1.7%至4204万令吉，受累于工程业务营业额下滑。   KUALA LUMPUR (June 24): Kinergy Advancement Bhd (KL:KAB) has inked a memorandum of understanding (MOU) with state-owned investment company Permodalan Kedah Bhd (PKB) to develop sustainable energy projects.   In a statement on Monday, KAB said it will take on the technical responsibilities and utilise its experience in the energy sector, while PKB will identify potential land for renewable energy projects and assist in authorisation responsibilities such as obtaining project licensing.   It said the partnership focuses on the deployment of clean energy, renewable energy, energy-efficient solutions projects and engineering works, among others. This includes the development of solar farms and waste-to-energy generation.   “Beyond this MOU lies a vision for accelerated growth aimed at transforming Kedah into a beacon of progress and innovation,” said KAB’s executive deputy chairman Datuk Lai Keng Onn.   “KAB values PKB’s dedication to advancing the state through innovative technologies that prioritise environmental impact reduction alongside achieving energy conservation, stability and resilience in line with Kedah’s sustainable development objectives,” he added.   The MOU will be valid for two years from its commencement date of June 19.   While it is not expected to have any material effect on KAB’s earnings for the financial year ending Dec 31, 2024 (FY2024), the group said it is expected to contribute positively to the group’s future earnings.   Lai is the largest shareholder in KAB with a 19.54% direct stake and a 9.23% indirect stake held through Stocqtech Sdn Bhd.   Other substantial shareholders include Tong Kum Loong with a 9.03% stake and a 1.59% indirect stake held through Regalis Investment Ltd and Gemini Bliss Sdn Bhd.   For the first quarter ended March 31, 2024 (1QFY2024), the group’s net profit doubled to RM4.97 million from RM2.43 million a year earlier due to higher contribution by its sustainable energy solutions segment.   Revenue declined by 1.7% year-on-year to RM42.04 million from RM42.76 million, dragged by its engineering segment which recorded lower revenue due to project completion.   Shares of KAB were up one sen to 37.5 sen at Monday’s noon break, valuing the group at RM744.9 million.

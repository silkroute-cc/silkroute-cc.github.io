## Body

马来西亚驻伊斯坦布尔总领事馆Tengku Mohd Dzaraif Raja Abdul Kadir（左起）、Desan Shipyard副主席Hasan Özgür Çilli及主席Cenk Ismail Kaptanoglu、马六甲首席部长Datuk Seri Ab Rauf Yusoh、林木生集团兼Leaptec Engineering私人有限公司集团董事经理拿督林福源及Leaptec总执行长Datuk Mohd Anis Hisham Abd Aziz。   （吉隆坡14日讯）产业发展商林木生集团（LBS Bina Group Bhd）的间接子公司Leaptec Engineering私人有限公司与土耳其公司Desan Deniz Ins San A S签署谅解备忘录（MOU），探讨在马六甲Tanjung Bruas发展造船厂的可行性。   林木生集团周五发布文告指出，双方预计在占地1200英亩（485.62公顷）的填海土地上，分3个阶段兴建造船厂，总值超过20亿令吉。   根据谅解备忘录，双方还将探讨获得相关部门的支持、补贴及长期融资等可能性。   谅解备忘录自2023年4月13日起生效，有效期为12个月，除非双方同意续签或达成最终协议。   林木生集团兼Leaptec主席丹斯里林福山说：“我们期待与Desan合作。在马六甲填海项目中，我们考虑了不同的合作伙伴，很高兴与Desan建立联系。Desan在造船厂建造业务、造船、船舶维修、维护、现代化、改装和翻新各类商业和海军舰艇方面享有盛名，我们相信这项业务将成为马六甲项目的合适增长行业。”   截稿时，林木生集团起1仙或2.35%，至43.5仙，市值为6亿8262万令吉。   KUALA LUMPUR (April 14): Property developer LBS Bina Group Bhd's indirect subsidiary Leaptec Engineering Sdn Bhd has entered into a memorandum of understanding (MOU) with Türkiye-based Desan Deniz Ins San A S to explore the feasibility of developing a shipyard in Tanjung Bruas, Melaka.   The proposed shipyard, to undertake ship building and ship repair works, is expected to be developed in three phases on 1,200 acres (485.62 hectares) of reclaimed land, with a value of over RM2 billion, said LBS Bina in a statement on Friday (April 14).   Under the MOU, both parties will also explore the possibilities of obtaining support from the relevant authorities, subsidies, and soft long-term finance, among others.   The MOU will be effective for 12 months from April 13, 2023, unless renewed by mutual agreement of the parties, or when both parties enter into a definitive agreement.   “We are looking forward to our collaboration with Desan. We have considered various partners to work with in developing our Melaka reclamation project and are pleased to connect with Desan. Desan is well known in the shipyard construction business, shipbuilding, ship repair, maintenance, modernisation, conversion and renovation of all types of commercial and naval ships, and we believe that this business will be a suitable growth industry for our Melaka project," said Tan Sri Lim Hock San, the chairman of LBS Bina and Leaptec.   This agreement follows Leaptec's reclamation and development deal with the Melaka government to reclaim 1,200 acres that fall under the 25,000-acre Melaka Waterfront Economic Zone along the state's coastline.   According to LBS Bina, Desan is a prominent Turkish shipyard solution provider that is chiefly involved in building and developing shipyards, maintaining and repairing ships, shipbuilding, offshore businesses, and other shipping-related activities.   It is capable of building and repairing naval vessels, all types of tankers, bulk carriers, tugboats, and yachts.

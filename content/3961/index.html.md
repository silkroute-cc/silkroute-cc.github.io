（吉隆坡10日讯）前进控股（Advancecon Holdings Bhd）的联号公司获得价值4548万令吉合约，负责建设和完成美里联合循环燃气轮机电站项目的土方工程和其他相关工程。   文告显示，前进控股持股30%联号公司Advancecon（砂拉越）私人有限公司（ASSB）接受了Petros Power Sendirian Bhd的决标信。   ASSB主要从事提供土方工程与土木工程服务，以及其他相关服务的业务。   合约期限为自决标信之日起12个月。该集团有意通过内部融资及/或外部贷款筹资。   KUALA LUMPUR (Aug 10): Advancecon Holdings Bhd’s associate has bagged a RM45.48 million award as the contractor for the proposed construction and completion of earthworks and other related works, in relation to the Miri combined cycle gas turbine power plant project.   In a bourse filing on Thursday (August 10), Advancecon said its 30%-owned associate company Advancecon (Sarawak) Sdn Bhd (ASSB) had accepted the letter of award (LOA) from Petros Power Sendirian Bhd for the project.   ASSB is principally involved in the business of providing earthworks and civil engineering services and other related services.   The contract period is 12 months from the date of LOA.

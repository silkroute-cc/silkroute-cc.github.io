又是一年中秋到，月到中秋分外明。    由中国文化和旅游部推出，海外中国文化中心、驻外旅游办事处共同主办的2023年“天涯共此时——中秋节”全球联动活动日前在马来西亚拉开帷幕。   今年中秋，吉隆坡中国文化中心将通过举办中秋园游会、中华文化全国行、数字主题资源网络展演展映等活动，与马来西亚民众共庆佳节，共同感受中华传统文化魅力。   9月30日，我中心将与本地商协会、旅行社、航空公司等合作，早10点至晚9点在士毛月Orchard Hill举办2023“天涯共此时·中秋艺起Fun”园游会。现场活动精彩不断， 除了贯穿全天的沉浸式传统文化工作坊，还有“但愿人长久”月光音乐会、在月色下提灯漫游Orchard Hill的月光游园会、中秋图片展、中秋小吃品尝、灯谜挑战以及各种文化体验、美食、抽奖等惊喜等您来探索。    该活动由马来西亚—中国总商会联办，马中民俗文旅总商会、珍珠假期（马）旅游有限公司为合作单位，中国南方航空为指定航空，并获Orchard Hill场地支持。 参与民众将有机会赢取马来西亚往返中国机票、旅游配套、酒店住宿、猫山王榴莲、定制巧克力等丰富奖品！            此外，中秋期间，我中心发起主办的“中华文化全国行“第二站，将于9月22日走进有600多年历史的吉兰丹州话望生布赖客家村，与当地学校师生和村民共度中秋。   活动期间，我中心还将通过网络平台，推出“天涯共此时——中秋节”活动主题歌《明月几时有》、“中秋明月·寻音自然”线上音乐会、“中秋明月·舞动自然”线上歌舞晚会以及“我在中国过中秋”系列 Vlog等一批内容丰富的主题活动资源，展示中秋传统文化习俗和中国当代发展面貌。   四海共明月，天涯共此时。今年中秋邀您再相聚，一起体验大马中秋的别样风情。                 9月30日中秋园游会文化工作坊、     “但愿人长久”月光音乐会报名      工作坊（一）    中秋剪纸｜2PM-4PM or 4PM-6PM   线上报名：https://forms.gle/4UTQJXEGqSXKUrcp9      工作坊（二）    捏面人｜2PM–4PM   线上报名：https://forms.gle/aMHM7eVNURvVRPD18      工作坊（三）    亲子冰皮月饼制作｜10AM-12PM or 4PM-6PM   线上报名：https://forms.gle/HbHcrfu4B1KTFgJd6      工作坊（四）    峇迪蜡染｜10AM-12PM or 4PM-6PM   线上报名：https://forms.gle/FNacvSNJJy2NUKWZ9      工作坊（五）    福袋拓印与制香｜10AM-12PM or 2PM-4PM   线上报名：https://forms.gle/7y6rXocRouCaDaNo9      “但愿人长久”月光音乐会 | 7PM-8PM    线上报名：https://forms.gle/HA8g3PQDgyM35Jkf6      Orchard Hill月光游园会 | 8PM-9PM    免费参加      工作坊报名截止日期：9月26日（星期二）3PM     参加工作坊者将获主办单位颁发参与证书，以资鼓励。     名额有限，先到先得！    主办单位保有最终修改、变更、活动解释及取消本活动之权利，恕不另行通知。   欲询问详情，可电邮至：   project@chinaculturalcentre.my 或WhatsApp +6019-6271048（邱先生）      JoinUs @ A Moonmoment to Remember - Mid-Autumn Festival 2023\!      As the Mid-Autumn Festival approaches, with the moon shining brightly, “A Moonmoment to Remember - Mid-Autumn Festival 2023” Global Celebrations, which initiated by the Ministry of Culture and Tourism of the People's Republic of China and jointly organized by overseas China Cultural Centres and China National Tourist Offices, was inaugurated in Malaysia."    "This year, during the Mid-Autumn Festival, the China Cultural Centre in Kuala Lumpur will celebrate the festival alongside the people of Malaysia. Through activities such as the Mid-Autumn under Moonlight Celebrations, the Chinese Culture Camp, and a digital-themed exhibition, we aim to provide an opportunity for everyone to experience the charm of traditional Chinese culture."   On September 30th, China Cultural Centre in Kuala Lumpur will collaborate with local NGO, travel agencies, airlines, and more to host the “A Moonmoment to Remember - Mid-Autumn Festival 2023” at Orchard Hill from 10AM to 9PM.   This event is jointly organized by the Malaysia-China Chamber of Commerce, in collaboration with the Malaysia-China Culture Folklore Tourism Association and Pearl Holiday (M) Travel &amp; Tour Sdn Bhd. It has the Official Airline Support of China Southern Airlines (Malaysia) and the Official Venue Support of Orchard Hill.   Participants in this event will have the opportunity to win exciting prizes, including round-trip air tickets from Malaysia to China, travel packages, hotel accommodations, Musang King durians, custom chocolates, and more\!   The event promises a rich array of activities throughout the day, including immersive traditional culture workshops. In the evening, there will be a special 'Wishing for Longevity' moonlight concert. Attendees can also enjoy moonlit strolls through Orchard Hill with lanterns, a Mid-Autumn photo exhibition, tasting of traditional Mid-Autumn snacks and mooncakes, lantern riddles, and a variety of cultural experiences, delicious cuisine, limited-edition gifts, and exciting lucky draws waiting for you to explore.   The Mid-Autumn Festival is an important traditional festival for the Chinese people, with ancient legends and touching stories, carrying the vision of reunion, harmony, and a better life.Let’s celebrate the Mid-Autumn Festival 2023 with us in a meaningful ways &amp; fun in learning\!

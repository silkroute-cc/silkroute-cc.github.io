上海氢枫能源和LHSB将在马来西亚开展氢能合作   Hydrexia, LHSB to collaborate on hydrogen in Malaysia    Kallanish （开阑商务信息咨询）报道，中国领先的氢气解决方案提供商上海氢枫能源技术有限公司最近与马来西亚LHSB公司签署一项技术合作和商业化协议。后者是马来西亚国有石油公司Petronas旗下的可再生能源和氢能部门。   Leading Chinese hydrogen solution provider Shanghai Hyfun Energy Technology (Hydrexia) has recently signed a technology collaboration and commercialisation agreement with Malaysia’s LHSB. The latter is the renewable energy and hydrogen arm of Malaysian state oil company Petronas, Kallanish  notes.   根据氢枫宣布的协议，两家公司将共同推动马来西亚沙捞越的氢能发展。两家公司将分阶段开展氢能技术商业化的研发工作。   Under the agreement announced by Hydrexia, the companies will jointly promote hydrogen development inSarawak, Malaysia. They will carry out research and development towards the commercialisation of hydrogen technologies in stages.    合作的主要领域包括氢的提纯、储存和运输，以及加氢站的建设和运营。   Key areas of cooperation include hydrogen purification, storage and transportation, as well as the construction and operation of hydrogen refuelling stations.   LHSB表示，其致力于开发先进的制氢设施，并向全球供应电解槽。该公司拥有一个电解槽组装和分销设施，为寻求氢气解决方案的各行各业提供支持，并促进其低碳目标的实现。   LHSB says it’s committed to developing advanced hydrogen production facilities and supplying electrolysers globally. It has an electrolyser assembly and distribution facility to support various industries seeking hydrogen solutions and promote their low-carbon goals.   氢枫将提供其在氢能技术、产品和跨产业链解决方案方面的专业能力，帮助探索氢能在不同场景中的应用潜力。目的是为本地氢能产业注入“新的活力”。   Hydrexia will provide its professional capabilities in hydrogen energy technology, products and cross-industry chain solutions to help explore the application potential of hydrogen in different scenarios. The aim is to inject “new vitality” into the local hydrogen industry.   氢枫东南亚公司首席执行官George Gan评论道：“我们很高兴能与LHSB建立战略合作伙伴关系。这不仅进一步扩大了我们在东南亚地区的业务拓展规模，也为我们在该地区的本地业务运营增加了重要的深度。与LHSB的合作使我们能够充分利用我们的氢能技术解决方案来满足马来西亚客户的需求。我们期待在未来扩大与LHSB的合作。”   “We are excited about forming this strategic partnership with LHSB. It not only further expands the scale of our business expansion in Southeast Asia, but also adds significant depth to our local business operation in this region,” comments George Gan, ceo of Hydrexia Southeast Asia. “The collaboration with LHSB enables us to best utilise our hydrogentechnology solutions to serve the needs of our customers in Malaysia. We look forward to expanding the cooperation with LHSB going forward.”   有关合作的细节尚未披露。   Details on the collaboration remain undisclosed.   转自：Kallanish国际钢铁资讯   来源： [阅读原文](<https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/BLTp1bmI2aRFpBlpC3XyjA>)

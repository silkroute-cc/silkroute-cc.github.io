（吉隆坡7日讯）大马统计局表示，我国2022年工业生产指数（IPI）增幅收窄至6.9%，2021年为7.2%，推动力来自制造业指数（8.2%）、电力指数（4.5%）及矿业指数（2.8%）。   大马统计局局长Datuk Seri Dr Mohd Uzir Mahidin指出，相对于2021年第四季，2022年末季IPI缓慢增长4.1%，得益于制造业（4%）及矿业（6.2%）指数增长。   他在文告中表示：“12月IPI增长3.0%，低于11月的4.8%，推动力来自矿业（4.1%）和制造业（3.0%）指数。”   他透露，2022年12月制造业产出增长了3.0%，主要受益于电器和电子产品（7.2%）、运输设备和其他制成品（8.5%）、食品、饮料和烟草产品（3.4%）以及非金属矿物产品、基本金属和金属制品（2.2%）。   石油、化工、橡胶和塑料产品（0.9%）以及纺织品、服装、皮革制品和鞋类（0.5%）的产量下降幅度较小。   同时，木制品、家具、纸制品和印刷品产量继续下降4.3%，而2022年11月的降幅为3.6%。   KUALA LUMPUR (Feb 7): Malaysia’s industrial production index (IPI) for 2022 narrowed to 6.9% from 7.2% recorded in 2021, driven by the manufacturing index (8.2%), electricity index (4.5%), and mining index (2.8%), according to the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM).   Chief statistician Datuk Seri Dr Mohd Uzir Mahidin said for the final quarter of 2022 (4Q2022), the index grew at a slower rate of 4.1% compared to 4Q2021, attributed to the growth in manufacturing (4%) and mining (6.2%) indices.   “In December, the index’s growth slipped to 3.0% from 4.8% recorded in November, driven by the mining index (4.1%) and manufacturing index (3.0%),” he said in a statement.   He said output in the manufacturing sector expanded by 3.0% in December 2022, driven by electrical and electronics products (7.2%), transport equipment and other manufactures (8.5%), food, beverages and tobacco products (3.4%), and non-metallic mineral products, basic metal and fabricated metal products (2.2%).   Smaller declines were recorded in the output of petroleum, chemical, rubber and plastic products (0.9%), and textiles, wearing apparel, leather products and footwear (0.5%).

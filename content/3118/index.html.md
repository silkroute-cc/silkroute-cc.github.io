（吉隆坡26日讯）Kerjaya Prospek Group Bhd表示，由子公司及Samsung C&amp;T Corp组成的财团赢得一份价值14亿5000万令吉的合同，为Texas Instruments Electronics Malaysia私人有限公司在马六甲兴建厂房。   根据文告，Kerjaya Prospek子公司Kerjaya Prospek（马）私人有限公司（KPM）与Samsung C&amp;T（KL）私人有限公司的Samsung-KP联营公司获得决标信，在马六甲自由贸易区峇株安南工业园（Taman Perindustrian Batu Berendam）兴建与完成TIEM2 Bump/Probe/AT厂房。   Samsung C&amp;T持有Samsung-KP联营公司的70%股权，其余30%股权由KPM持有。   Kerjaya Prospek透露，该项目将于今年11月21日动工，预计将在28个月内竣工。   集团预计，该项目将在未来2、3年内，为集团提供额外收入来源。   截至周三休市，Kerjaya Prospek企于1.19令吉，成交量为1万股。   KUALA LUMPUR (Oct 26): Kerjaya Prospek Group Bhd said a consortium comprising its unit and Samsung C&amp;T Corp has bagged a contract from Texas Instruments Electronics Malaysia Sdn Bhd to build a factory in Melaka for RM1.45 billion.   In a filing with Bursa Malaysia on Wednesday (Oct 26), Kerjaya Prospek said the joint venture (JV) between its unit Kerjaya Prospek (M) Sdn Bhd (KPM) and Samsung C&amp;T (KL) Sdn Bhd had received a letter of award to build and complete TIEM2 bump/probe/AT factory construction at Taman Perindustrian Batu Berendam, Free Trade Zone, Melaka.   Samsung C&amp;T has a 70% stake in the JV, while KPM holds the remaining 30%.   Kerjaya Prospek said the project will commence on Nov 21, and be completed within 28 months.   It said the contract is expected to provide an additional stream of revenue for the group over the next two to three years.

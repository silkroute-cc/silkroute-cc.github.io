（吉隆坡3日讯）敬业（Kejuruteraan Asastera Bhd）获得国油气体（Petronas Gas Bhd）子公司的合约，承接沙巴52兆瓦发电厂及其相关设施的工程、采购、建设与调试（EPCC）。   敬业今日向大马交易所报备，子公司KAB Energy Holdings私人有限公司接获国油气体子公司Regas Terminal (Lahad Datu)私人有限公司的决标信，该发电厂位于实必丹油气工业园，将是国家石油（Petronas）浮式液化天然气（ZLNG）项目的唯一能源供应来源。   该公司指出，ZLNG将是沙巴首座近海天然气设施，每年最低产能为200万吨。   该合约总价为2亿3000万令吉。   发电厂的EPCC工程将于今年第二季开始，并计划于2026年首季完成。   休市时，敬业平盘挂于39.5仙，共701万股成交。   KUALA LUMPUR (Jan 3): Kejuruteraan Asastera Bhd has secured a letter of award (LOA) from Petronas Gas Bhd's (PetGas) unit Regas Terminal (Lahad Datu) Sdn Bhd for the engineering, procurement, construction, and commissioning (EPCC) of a 52-megawatt (MW) power plant and its associated facilities in Sabah.   In a statement to Bursa Malaysia on Tuesday (Jan 3), Kejuruteraan Asastera said the LOA awarded to its unit KAB Energy Holdings Sdn Bhd is for a power plant located at the Sipitang Oil and Gas Industrial Park and will be the sole source of energy supply to Petronas’ upcoming floating liquefied natural gas (ZLNG) project.   It said the ZLNG plant, slated to be Sabah’s first near-shore natural gas facility, has a minimum capacity of two million tonnes per annum.   The contract is for a total non-escalating all-inclusive fixed lump sum of RM230 million.   Kejuruteraan Asastera managing director Datuk Lai Keng Onn said securing the project award from PetGas amplifies Kejuruteraan Asastera’s sustainable energy initiatives undertaken since 2019.   "The power plant, upon completion, will be the single largest gas engine plant in the country, and serve as a crucial power source for Petronas’ upcoming ZLNG plant.   "Furthermore, our joint feasibility studies with PGB (PetGas) show that the power plant is reliable and will achieve significant carbon reduction,” he said.   EPCC works for the power plant will commence in the second quarter of 2023 and is targeted to be completed in the first quarter of 2026.

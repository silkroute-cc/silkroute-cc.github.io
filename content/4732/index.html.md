UEM阳光携手伙伴在柔州建可再生能源工业园区     ITRAMAS董事经理Lee Choo Boo（前排左起）、UEM阳光总执行长Sufian Abdullah和CMEC总裁助理Li Mingqiang，以及ITRAMAS项目发展董事Royd Lee（后排左起）、UEM Lestra代总执行长Harman Faiz、UEM集团主席Datuk Amran Hafiz Affifudin、CMEC总经理方彦水、UEM阳光策略与转型总监Irwin Mohd Eusoff、CMEC第五工程成套事业部赵文科, 无锡CMEC主席Xu Hong及CMEC第五工程成套事业部副经理Hu Jianhui。   （吉隆坡16日讯）UEM阳光（UEM Sunrise Bhd，KL:UEMS）及合作伙伴，计划在柔佛州依斯干达公主城（Iskandar Puteri）发展一个占地40英亩的可再生能源工业园区。   根据今日发布的联合文告，UEM阳光、ITRAMAS Corp私人有限公司和中国机械设备工程股份有限公司（CMEC）签署了一项协议，将打造一个园区，旨在吸引中国制造商和供应商，涵盖可再生能源和电动汽车价值链，以及高科技研究公司。   该园区是UEM阳光大股东UEM集团（UEM Group Bhd）主导的1吉瓦混合太阳能发电厂项目的一部分，也是国家能源转型路线图（NETR）的旗舰项目之一。UEM集团由国库控股（Khazanah Nasional Bhd）独资拥有。   UEM集团董事经理Datuk Mohd Izani Ghani表示：“与中国伙伴的合作，不仅提高了这个国家策略项目的可行性和影响力，也突显跨境合作在推动可持续发展方面的重要性。”   根据文告，该园区将设有可再生能源中心，包括太阳能组件工厂和研究设施，以及其他设施，旨在促进大马能源转型和电动汽车生态系统的发展。   ITRAMAS董事经理Lee Choo Boo指出，此次合作不仅强化了公司对推动大马能源行业发展的承诺，也凸显了其为可持续发展所做的集体努力。   国机工程集团总经理方彦水表示，该集团将与UEM集团及ITRAMAS建立长期合作关系，重点关注可再生能源、工业园区、基础设施和数据中心。   “他们的目标是吸引中国潜在可再生能源和电动汽车制造商。我们在新能源、数据中心建设与营运、仓储，以及智能物流方面的经验符合大马合作伙伴的利益。”   KUALA LUMPUR (May 16): Property developer UEM Sunrise Bhd (KL:UEMS) and its partners plan to develop a 40-acre renewable energy industrial park in Iskandar Puteri, Johor.   UEM Sunrise, ITRAMAS Corp Sdn Bhd and China Machinery Engineering Corp (CMEC) signed a pact to develop a park “strategically designed to attract Chinese manufacturers and suppliers” across the renewable energy and electric vehicle (EV) value chains, as well as high-tech research firms, according to a joint statement.   The park is part of the one-gigawatt hybrid solar power plant project led by UEM Sunrise's majority shareholder UEM Group Bhd, which in turn is wholly owned by Khazanah Nasional Bhd, and is one of the flagship catalytic projects under Malaysia’s National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR).   “Securing partnership interest from China not only enhances the viability and impact of this national strategic project but also underscores the importance of cross-border collaboration in driving sustainable development,” said UEM Group managing director Datuk Mohd Izani Ghani.   The industrial park will feature a renewable energy hub, housing a solar module factory and research facilities, among other amenities, aimed at spurring the growth of Malaysia’s energy transition and EV ecosystems, the statement noted.   For ITRAMAS, the partnership not only reinforces the company's commitment to advancing Malaysia's energy sector but also highlights its collective efforts towards sustainable development, said its managing director Lee Choo Boo.   "We are particularly excited about the RE (renewable energy) hub within the park,” he said. “The facilities will not only enhance our production capabilities but also propel us to the forefront of innovation in renewable energy.”   CMEC, meanwhile, will work closely with UEM Group and ITRAMAS in a long-term partnership, with a focus on renewable energy, industrial parks, infrastructure, and data centres, said its general manager Fang Yanshui.   "Their goal is to attract potential RE and EV manufacturers from China,” Fang continued, “CMEC’s experience in new energy, data centre construction and operation, warehousing, and smart logistics aligns with the interests of Malaysian partners.”   At 3.02pm, shares of UEM Sunrise were trading down one sen or 0.85% at RM1.16 after 5.51 million shares changed hands.

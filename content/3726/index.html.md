学习中文，为你的人生添加新的色彩！新学期汉语课程接受报名，让我们一起学习美丽的语言——汉语！   无论你是初学者还是已经掌握了一定程度的汉语，新学期HSK培训班（1-3级）等你报名！HSK培训班由马来亚大学孔子学院李禹哲老师授课。李老师擅长互动式教学，以幽默活泼的教学方式为学员定制教学内容。上课使用的HSK教材是以自然幽默的风格、亲切熟悉的话题设计的新型汉语教材，帮助你轻松学习汉语。我们提供小班教学，让每位学生都能得到充分的关注和指导。无论您是为了学术、事业、还是出于兴趣学习汉语，欢迎您报名参加HSK课程。   Learn Chinese and explore new experiences in your life\! The new semester of Chinese Language courses is now open for registration. Let's learn the beautiful language of Chinese together\!    Whether you are a beginner or have already mastered a certain level of Chinese, the HSK class (levels 1-3) is waiting for you to register\! The HSK training classes will be taught by Teacher Li Yuzhe from the Kong Zi Institute Universiti Malaya. Teacher Li is good at interactive teaching and customizes teaching content for students with a humorous and lively teaching style. The HSK textbooks used in class are designed with a natural and humorous style and familiar topics, making it easy for you to learn Chinese. We offer small class teaching, so that every student can receive sufficient attention and guidance. Whether you are learning Chinese for academic, career, or personal interest purposes, you are welcome to participate in the HSK course.

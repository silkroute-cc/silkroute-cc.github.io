据马来西亚航空消息， 7月28日起，马航吉隆坡-广州航线实施5天4夜行前闭环管理措施，适用于从2022年8月1日起乘坐MH376由吉隆坡国际机场飞往广州白云机场的所有乘客。    隔离酒店为：Eastin Hotel Kuala Lumpur   隔离费用为：1700令吉起   马来西亚航空公司网址：www.malaysiaairlines.com   具体要求和规定请阅读（中文）：        Entry Requirements to China     Malaysia Airlines Implements    ‘Pre-Departure Health Screening Programme’    for Passengers Travelling via Flight MH376 from Kuala Lumpur (KUL) to Guangzhou (CAN)    As part of our efforts to protect and ensure passenger safety, prevent further spread of the COVID-19 virus, and ensure seamless travel experiences for our passengers, Malaysia Airlines will be implementing a dedicated   ‘Pre-Departure Health Screening Programme’   applicable to all passengers on flight MH376 departing from Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL) to Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN) effective 1 August 2022.   The Pre-Departure Health Screening Programme is an initiative implemented by the airline as an added layer of precaution in facilitating seamless travel to China and is  compulsory  for all Malaysia Airlines’ passengers departing to China from Kuala Lumpur.   All passengers travelling via flight MH376 will need to undergo a 5-days/4-nights, Pre-Departure Health Screening Programme’ at the designated hotel accommodation by the airline’s service provider and COVID-19 tests as regulated by the Chinese Embassy in the host country and Malaysia Airlines.    Health Requirements and Passenger Flow    Below are the 6-steps requirement under the Pre-Departure Health Screening Programme, which will take effect as early as five (5) days before the scheduled departure to China.   To ensure smooth processing and communications, the entire process within the Programme will be facilitated by a service provider in close collaboration with the airline.    Below is a sample scenario of a passenger scheduled to depart on 1 August 2022.    Steps | Day(s) before scheduled departure | Process / Testing Requirements | Remarks 1 | 5 days before scheduled departure (Example: 27 July 2022) | Passenger is required to take the first RT-PCR test 24 hours before checking-in at the designated Hotel from a testing institution recommended by the Chinese Embassy in Malaysia.Note: If the Departure is on 1 August 2022, the RT-PCR test must be taken on 27 July before 12:00 pm.Please click here for the list of verified testing institutions. 2 | 4 days before scheduled departure (Example: 28 July 2022) | Passenger must submit their RT-PCR test results to the airline’s dedicated service provider at point of hotel check in.Hotel check-in (2.30pm to 5.30pm LT) | Positive RT-PCR: Passenger will not be allowed to check-in hotel. 3 | 3 days before scheduled departure (Example: 29 July 2022) 4 | 2 days before scheduled departure (Example: 30 July 2022) | Passenger must undergo the second RT-PCR test at the hotel, taken 48 hours prior to departure, as required by the authorities. | Positive RT-PCR: Passenger will be requested to check-out from the hotel. 5 | 1 day before scheduled departure (Example: 31 July 2022) | Passenger must undergo the third RT-PCR test at the hotel, taken 24 hours prior to departure, as required by the authorities.The RT-PCR results will be submitted by the passenger as part of the requirements in obtaining the Health Declaration Certificate (HDC) from the Chinese Embassy prior to travel. | Positive RT-PCR:Passenger will be requested to check-out from the hotel. 6 | The day of departure (Example: 1 August 2022) | Passenger is required to take a RT-Antigen test 12 hours before departure.Passenger is recommended to wear Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) prior to Hotel check-out and transfer to airport.Passenger will check-out of the Hotel/Accommodation.Passenger will be transferred from the Hotel to KLIA for check-in and other requirements prior to boarding.Health Code Checking will be conducted by the airline personnel to ensure the necessary approval has been obtained for passenger travel. | Negative RT-Antigen: Proceed for check-in process    Important:  Passengers who are detected positive for COVID-19 within the Programme duration  will not be allowed to continue their travel .    Packages Offered    The Programme package starts from MYR 1,700 for 5-days, 4-night stay per passenger. The price is inclusive of but not limited to:     Hotel accommodation   Meals (Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner)   COVID-19 test(s)   Transfer services from hotel to KLIA   Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) set      Dedicated Check-In Counters    Passengers travelling on flight MH376 will be escorted by the service provider and to proceed to the dedicated check-in counters located at Level 5, Departure Hall, KLIA.

（吉隆坡18日讯）柏卡集团（Pekat Group Bhd）公布，获得一份价值1亿1500万令吉的合约，承接霹雳州近打县一座29.99兆瓦太阳能发电厂的工程、采购、建设与调试（EPCC）工作。   该集团向大马交易所报备，上述合约由美佳第一（Mega First Corp Bhd）间接独资子公司MF Solar Tronoh私人有限公司，授予其独资子公司Pekat Solar私人有限公司。   该项目预计从2024年第四季开始，商业营运日期定于2025年12月31日。   这是柏卡今年获得的第二份大型太阳能EPCC合约。   KUALA LUMPUR (Sept 18): Pekat Group Bhd said it has secured a contract worth RM115 million to develop a 29.99MW (megawatt) solar farm in Kinta, Perak.    The contract was awarded by MF Solar Tronoh Sdn Bhd, an indirect wholly owned unit of Mega First Corp Bhd to Pekat Solar Sdn Bhd, the company said in a filing.    Pekat Solar, a wholly owned unit of Pekat Group, will undertake engineering, procurement, construction and commissioning (EPCC) works for the project, which was secured by MF Solar Tronoh under the Corporate Green Power Programme (CGPP).   The project is expected to start in 4Q2024, with commercial operation date (COD) set at Dec 31, 2025.    This is the second large-scale-solar EPCC project award secured by Pekat this year.    Recent solar-related projects won by Pekat include a power-purchase agreement in Maldives, estimated at US$6.9 million, to provide electricity for 10 years from a 1.73MWp (megawatt peak) solar system, with 1.4MWh (megawatt hour) battery storage.   Other projects won by Pekat include the RM21.79 million earthing and lightning protection works for Gamuda Bhd’s data centre project.    Shares of Pekat rose half a sen or 0.53% to 94 sen at noon market break on Wednesday, valuing the group at RM606.27 million and 39 times trailing 12-months price-to-earnings ratio.

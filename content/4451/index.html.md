“你好！中国”——中马50·以心相交绘画比赛得奖作品展即日起至3月30日开放民众参观！     本次展览共展出“你好！中国”——中马50·以心相交绘画比赛中的60幅得奖作品。这些作品题材丰富、形式多样，个性鲜明，体现中国与马来西亚50年友谊的印证。主办单位冀望通过本次展览，以艺术和文化的结合推进青少年的人文交流，增进青少年对两国文化和旅游的了解，缔结全新的羁绊，夯实中马友好的基础。     “你好！中国”——中马50·以心相交绘画比赛由吉隆坡中国文化中心主办，雪隆海南会馆（天后宫）联办，马来西亚旅游、艺术和文化部及中国驻马来西亚使馆为支持单位，厦门航空为指定航空。赛事于去年8月在吉隆坡成功举行，吸引近300名马来西亚中小学与大专院校生参加，一展出众的美术绘画才艺。     展期：即日起至2024年3月30日   地点：吉隆坡中国文化中心   时间：周一至周六，上午10时至下午4时（周日与公共假期不开放）   备注：免费入场，毋需预约。   欲了解更多详情，请浏览www.chinaculturalcentre.my 或通过以下方式咨询：   电邮：event@chinaculturalcentre.my   电话：+603-2181 2456 / +6019-627 1048 (WhatsApp only)     "Nihao\! China” - 50th Anniversary of Malaysia-China Diplomatic Relations Heart-to-heart Connection Drawing Contest - Winner Artworks Exhibition is officially open to the public for viewing from now till March 30.   The exhibition displayed 60 winning artworks from the contest. These works, with diverse themes and distinctive styles, serve as a testament to the 50-year friendship between Malaysia and China. The organizer hopes that through this exhibition, the combination of art and culture will promote humanistic exchanges among young people, enhance their understanding of the cultures and tourism of both countries, forge new bonds, and strengthen the foundation of Malaysia-China friendship..   "Nihao\! China” - 50th Anniversary of Malaysia-China Diplomatic Relations Heart-to-heart Connection Drawing Contest is organized by the China Cultural Centre in Kuala Lumpur and in collaboration with the Selangor and Federal Territory Hainan Association (Thean Hou Temple), supported by the Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture of Malaysia and the Embassy of the People’s Republic of China in Malaysia, Xiamen Airlines as the Official Airline. The event attracted nearly 300 primary and secondary school students to participate as well as college students.

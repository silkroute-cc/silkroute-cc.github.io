由中共中央宣传部、中华人民共和国文化和旅游部等联合主办的第二十届中国（深圳）国际文化产业博览交易会于2024年5月23日至27日在深圳国际会展中心举办。   本届文博会设置共设8个展馆，总面积为16万平方米。分别为：文化产业综合展（A馆、B馆）、粤港澳大湾区文化产业创新展、文旅融合·文化消费展、影视·出版·版权·游戏电竞展、非遗·老字号·工艺美术展、艺术·设计·国潮展、国际文化贸易展，共组织了6015家政府组团、文化机构和企业线上线下参展；吸引了来自60个国家和地区、302家海外展商线上线下参展；组织邀请108个国家和地区、3万余名海外专业观众线上线下参会。展会将展出文化产品超过12万件，4000多个文化产业投融资项目在现场进行展示与交易。   更多信息请关注第二十届中国（深圳）国际文化产业博览交易会官方网站http://www.cnicif.com/   The 20th China (Shenzhen) International Cultural Industries Fair   The 20th China (Shenzhen) International Cultural Industries Fair (hereinafter referred to as “ICIF”), jointly hosted by the Publicity Department of the CPC Central Committee, the Ministry of Culture and Tourism of the People’s Republic of China, etc., is scheduled to be held at the Shenzhen World Exhibition &amp; Convention Center from May 23 to 27, 2024.   The 20th ICIF consists of eight exhibition halls with a total area of 160,000 square meters, including Cultural Industries Halls A &amp; B, the GHM Greater Bay Area Hall, Cultural Tourism and Consumption Hall, TV and Film · Publishing · Copyright · E-sports Hall, Intangible Cultural Heritage · Time-honored Brand · Arts and Crafts Hall, Art · Design · Trendy Toys Hall, and International Cultural Trade Hall. The event organizes 6,015 governmental delegations, cultural institutions and enterprises to participate in online and offline exhibitions. Besides, it attracts 302 overseas exhibitors from 60 countries and regions to attend online and offline exhibitions, and invites more than 30,000 professional visitors from 108 countries and regions. More than 120,000 cultural products as well as over 4,000 cultural investment and financing projects will be displayed or traded at the event.

根据工作安排，中国驻马来西亚使馆领事服务大厅于10月27日全天和10月28日上午暂停对外办公，咨询电话及咨询邮箱正常服务。请申请人据此合理安排业务办理日期。有关咨询方式详见 [《关于证件办理和领保咨询方式的通知》](<https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzU5MzQxNTg4NA%3D%3D&amp;mid=2247497345&amp;idx=1&amp;sn=f8baf865e237a8b85ae122b030ef3142&amp;scene=21#wechat_redirect>) 。中国驻马来西亚大使馆2025年10月24日 The Consular Office of the Embassy of the People’s Republic of China in Malaysia will be closed on the 27th and the morning of 28th, October, 2025. You can reach us through our hotline and email for service requests during then. The office will reopen at 2:30 pm，28th October, 2025.Embassy of the People’s Republic of China in MalaysiaOctober 24, 2025

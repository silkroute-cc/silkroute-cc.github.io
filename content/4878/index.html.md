IJM集团总执行长兼董事经理李振辉表示，IJM参与数据中心建设市场，表明集团致力于创新和适应，以满足高科技行业的需求。（摄影：Sam Fong/The Edge）   （吉隆坡26日讯）IJM Corp Bhd（KL:IJM）获得价值3亿3170万令吉合同，为马电讯（Telekom Malaysia Bhd）（KL:TM）子公司在柔佛依斯干达公主城（Iskandar Puteri）设计及建造数据中心。   IJM在文告中表示，TM Technology Services私人有限公司将名为“依斯干达公主城数据中心2号楼”（Block 2 of the Iskandar Puteri Data Centre）的项目授予其独资子公司IJM Construction私人有限公司。   IJM说：“该数据中心将于2024年7月开始建设，预计于2025年第三季竣工。”   IJM集团总执行长兼董事经理李振辉表示，在云计算迅速增长以及对强大、可扩展基建设施需求的推动下，数据中心行业正在迅速扩张。   “我们参与数据中心建设市场，表明我们致力于创新和适应，以满足高科技行业的需求。”   “该数据中心项目是IJM Construction的一个策略里程碑，使我们的投资组合更加多元化，并增强了我们在高科技建筑方面的能力。”   该集团预计，此次扩张将为IJM Construction带来巨大的增长潜力。   KUALA LUMPUR (June 26): IJM Corp Bhd (KL:IJM) has secured a contract to design and build a data centre for a subsidiary of Telekom Malaysia Bhd (KL:TM) in Iskandar Puteri, Johor, for RM331.7 million.   The project — known as Block 2 of the Iskandar Puteri Data Centre — was secured from TM Technology Services Sdn Bhd via its wholly owned IJM Construction Sdn Bhd, IJM said in a statement on Wednesday.   "Construction of the data centre will begin in July 2024 and is slated for completion by the third quarter of 2025," said IJM.   The data centre aims to achieve Tier III certification under the Uptime Institute's Tier Classification System, which is the international standard for data centre performance. A Tier III certification means the data centre's maintenance can be done without downtime due to its redundant systems.   “The data centre sector is rapidly expanding, driven by the exponential growth of cloud computing and the need for robust, scalable infrastructure. Our involvement in the data centre construction market signifies our commitment to innovation and adaptability in meeting the needs of high-tech industries," said IJM group chief executive officer and managing director Lee Chun Fai.   "This data centre project is a strategic milestone for IJM Construction, diversifying our portfolio and enhancing our capabilities in high-tech construction,” Lee added.   The group expects this expansion to provide significant growth potential for IJM Construction.   This latest contract, combined with the RM962 million worth of jobs that IJM secured last week for a logistics hub in Shah Alam and a semiconductor manufacturing facility in Penang, have boosted IJM construction’s order book to RM7.3 billion.   "By leveraging its internal supply chain capabilities, including spun piles and industrialised building systems from IJM’s industry division, the group is well-positioned to capitalise on these opportunities.

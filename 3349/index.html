JGC和三星重工获EPCC合同 建马国油第三个浮式液化天然气项目

韩国三星重工时隔三年再获15亿美元海工订单

韩国三星重工12月22日宣布收到一家亚洲船东的海洋工程生产装备订单。合同金额为1.9611万亿韩元（约合15亿美元），占其2021年销售额的29.6%，此次承接的海工装备将于2027年8月交付。这是三星重工时隔3年首次承接海工订单。

三星重工没有透露具体的客户名称。但据航运媒体《贸易风》近日报道，日本日挥控股株式会社（JGC）和韩国三星重工已获得EPCC合同，将建造马来西亚国家石油公司的第三个浮式液化天然气（FLNG）项目。该项目的最低产能为200万吨/年，将使马来西亚国家石油公司的浮式液化天然气设施的液化天然气产量从270万吨/年提高到470万吨/年。

Japan's JGC wins 2nd floating LNG order from Malaysia's Petronas

TOKYO -- Japanese engineering group JGC Holdings has won its second order to build a floating liquefied natural gas production base for Malaysian state-run energy company Petronas as demand for LNG grows worldwide.

JGC will partner with Samsung Heavy Industries for the project in the eastern state of Sabah, with completion planned for 2027. The platform is expected to be one of the largest of its kind in the world.

Floating LNG facilities -- which produce LNG from natural gas and transfer it to carriers for shipping -- offer a mobility advantage that their onshore counterparts lack. The platforms can be moved from gas field to gas field as needed, depending on output.

JGC Corp. will be responsible for engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) of the production system, whose annual output will exceed 2 million tonnes. The order is estimated to be worth more than 100 billion yen ($753 million) for JGC.

Samsung Heavy will be the EPC provider for the vessel supporting the production system.

JGC won its first floating LNG plant project from Petronas in 2014. A Mozambique project awarded in 2017 began LNG shipments this November. JGC won a similar order in Nigeria this year.

LNG production is expected to rise worldwide as buyers of the fuel seek to diversify their suppliers after the shock of Russia's invasion of Ukraine. LNG demand was already rising as an alternative to coal, which emits more carbon dioxide when burned.

JGC Holdings has received about 600 billion yen in orders for the fiscal year through this coming March. The latest order brings it a step closer to meeting its 840 billion yen target for that year.

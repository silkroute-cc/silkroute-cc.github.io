工作坊：清韵·宋代山水小品｜Song Dynasty Landscape Painting

为了推广中国传统绘画艺术、提高对宋人山水小品审美与技法的认识，吉隆坡中国文化中心携手澄怀堂艺术中心与无痕馆举办"清韵"宋代山水小品工作坊，激发创意，融入古韵！

日期：2023年12月17日（星期日）

时间：上午10时 – 下午4时

地点：吉隆坡中国文化中心

导师：郑又硕（马来西亚书画导师）、符芳俊（马来西亚书画导师）

参与对象：凡15岁以上感兴趣者皆可报名参与

工作坊亮点：

★ 宋代山水小品概说与赏析；

★基础技法解析与练习（树法、石法、渲染法）；

★ 宋代经典山水小品临摹指导。

报名链接：https://forms.gle/vFZ51k4nj1eGpaTG7

费用：RM130（原价RM260，由中国文化中心补贴，含材料费与餐费）

🏆 所有参与者将获颁发参与证书！名额有限，先到先得，额满即止。

与我们一同踏上清韵之旅，让艺术点亮你的心灵，品味宋代山水小品的千年韵味！

Song Dynasty Landscape Painting Workshop #CallingForRegister

In order to promote Traditional Chinese Painting and elevate appreciation for Song Dynasty landscape art, the China Cultural Centre in Kuala Lumpur will in collaboration with Clarity Art Centre and Wu-Hen Oriental Arts, proudly presents the Song Dynasty Landscape Painting Workshop, this is an opportunity to stimulate creativity and immerse in ancient aesthetics!

Date: December 17, 2023 (Sunday)

Time: 10 am – 4 pm

Venue: China Cultural Centre in Kuala Lumpur

Instructors: Jordant Chang & Foo Fung Chun

Target Participants: Open to all enthusiasts aged 15 and above

Workshop Highlights:

★An overview and appreciation of landscape sketches of the Song Dynasty;

★Analysis and practice of basic techniques (Tree method, Stone method, Rendering method);

★Guidance on copying classic landscape sketches from the Song Dynasty.

Registration Form:

https://forms.gle/vFZ51k4nj1eGpaTG7

Fee: RM130 (Normal price RM260, 50% rebate by CCCKL, included materials and lunch)

🏆All participants will receive a Certificate of Participation!

Limited slots available, first come first served basis!

Let’s embark on the journey of art with us and savor the millennium charm of Song Dynasty landscape art!

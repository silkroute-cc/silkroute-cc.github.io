“中马50·以心相交” 绘画比赛报名倒计时！

本次比赛系我中心“你好中国！”品牌系列活动之一，适逢明年中马建交50周年，活动以“中马50·以心相交”为主题，将于2023年8月26日（周六）在雪隆海南会馆（天后宫）举办。

Have you registered for the "Nihao, China! - 50th Anniversary of Malaysia-China Diplomatic Relations · Heart-to-Heart Connection”Drawing Contest? Last chance to sign up! Act quickly and seize the opportunity!

The "Nihao, China! - 50th Anniversary of Malaysia-China Diplomatic Relations · Heart-to-Heart Connection” Drawing Contest will take place on Saturday, August 26, 2023, at the Thean Hou Temple in Kuala Lumpur.

比赛当天，主办方准备了丰富的节目和文化体验，所有参赛者还有机会参与幸运抽奖！除了多份精美礼品，本次赛事指定航空——厦门航空（Xiamen Airlines Malaysia）还将分别送出四张吉隆坡至福州/厦门/重庆往返机票！

On the day of the competition, the organizer has prepared a diverse range of programs and cultural experiences. All participants will also have the chance to participate in a lucky draw! In addition to multiple exquisite gifts, the designated airline for this event, Xiamen Airlines Malaysia, will be giving away four round-trip tickets from Kuala Lumpur to Fuzhou/Xiamen/Chongqing! Could you be one of the lucky winners?!

报名链接：

https://www.chinaculturalcentre.my/cn-heart-to-heart-connection-drawing-contest/

Register and join us NOW

https://www.chinaculturalcentre.my/en/heart-to-heart-connection-drawing-contest/

比赛设有四个组别：小学组（低年级）、小学组(高年级）、中学组和大专组。我们诚邀小朋友和大朋友一同参与，为明年中马建交50周年预热，为增进中马传统情谊添砖加瓦。

The contest will be divided into four (4) groups: Primary school - Junior Group, Primary school - Senior Group, Secondary School Group, and Uni-College Group (University and College Students) with Champion, 1st Runner-up, 2nd Runner-up, 3rd Runner-up, 4th Runner-up and Consolation prizes respectively. There are all kinds of additional prizes awaiting you! Furthermore, the winning artworks stand a chance to exhibit at the China Cultural Centre in Kuala Lumpur!

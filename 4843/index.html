“共栖”——气候危机下的生态艺术行动，即将开幕

为践行助力绿色发展，促进中马两国文化交流，吉隆坡中国文化中心邀请到四川美术学院李川教授暨实验艺术学院院长与靳立鹏副教授于6月19日至20日在吉隆坡举办“共栖”——气候危机下的生态艺术行动系列活动。

本次活动由中外文化交流中心、吉隆坡中国文化中心与四川美术学院联合主办，四川美术学院实验艺术学院承办，并获马来西亚艺术学院成为合作单位。本次活动围绕着“共栖”——气候危机下的生态艺术，在马来西亚开展一连串相关主题展览、学术论坛暨研讨会及生态工坊等文化交流活动。

欢迎民众报名出席活动开幕式，并前往中心观展！

“共栖”——气候危机下的生态艺术行动开幕式

活动详情

日期：19/6/2024（周三）

时间：3:00 PM - 4:30 PM

地点：吉隆坡中国文化中心

报名链接：https://forms.gle/2Q1vVTzLKrq9BsnN9

“Co-inhabitation” Eco-art Actions on the Climate Crisis Event Opening Soo

To promote green development and enhance cultural exchange between China and Malaysia, China Cultural Centre in Kuala Lumpur has invited Professor Li Chuan, Dean of the New Media Art Department at Sichuan Fine Arts Institute, and Associate Professor Jin Lipeng, to hold the the "Co-inhabitation: Eco-Art Action on the Climate Crisis'' Event from June 19th to 20th in Kuala Lumpur.

Co-organized by the China Culture Centre in Kuala Lumpur, the Sichuan Fine Arts Institute, and supported by the Malaysia Institute of Art. The event will feature a series of academic forums, seminars, and ecological workshops focusing on "Coexistence" and ecological art under climate crisis themes in Malaysia.

The Opening Ceremony of the “Co-inhabitation: Eco-Art Action on the Climate Crisis” event will be held on June 19th, 2024 (Wednesday), 3 PM at the event hall of China Cultural Centre in Kuala Lumpur .

The public is warmly invited to attend the opening ceremony and visit the exhibition at the CCCKL.

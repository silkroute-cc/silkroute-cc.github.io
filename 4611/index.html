2024学年第二期艺术课程，开始报名！

吉隆坡中国文化中心2024学年第二期艺术课程将于5月4日陆续开课，诚邀探索艺术之美。

在这里，你可以尽情感受古筝的悠扬旋律、发现中国传统书法的韵味，还可以领略工笔画的精湛技艺。不仅如此，你还将有机会在国画的世界中感受中国传统绘画艺术的魅力，或者在围棋的棋盘上挑战对手的智慧与策略。

即刻报名可享早鸟优惠，详情请扫描海报二维码。

Explore Artistic Beauty with China Cultural Centre in Kuala Lumpur!

China Cultural Centre in Kuala Lumpur is once again launching its highly anticipated series of art classes!

You can fully immerse yourself in the melodious tunes of the guzheng, explore the charm of traditional Chinese calligraphy, and appreciate the exquisite techniques of fine brushwork painting. Moreover, you will have the opportunity to experience the allure of Chinese traditional painting in the realm of Chinese painting or challenge opponents' intelligence and strategies on the Weiqi (Go) board.

Fostering communication and interaction among students through art courses, enabling them to collectively explore and cultivate the joy of art with fellow art enthusiasts. The second phase of the 2024 academic year's series art courses will commence on May 4th. Register now with just one click! Outstanding students also have the chance to participate in the "Cultural China Tour" program.

2026 Semester 3 Series of Arts Courses

As the season gently turns, it is the perfect time to pick up an old passion—or discover something new.

Let the sound of the guzheng bring calm, let the game of Go inspire thoughtful reflection, and let brush and ink create a moment of tranquillity amidst the rhythm of everyday life.

The 2026 Semester 3 Series of Arts Courses is now open for registration, with classes commencing from September 12.

Whether you are continuing your learning journey or experiencing these traditional arts for the first time, we warmly welcome you to the China Cultural Centre in Kuala Lumpur—to explore the arts, nurture your creativity, and find your own sense of balance.

✨ Courses Offered

Guzheng｜Go｜Chinese Color Painting｜Ink Painting｜Calligraphy｜Gongbi Painting

📢 Open to beginners and learners with prior experience

报名 | “马来西亚-中国清真产业论坛”诚邀企业参加

Malaysia-China Halal Business Conference

马来西亚-中国清真产业论坛

Halal Development Corporation (HDC), agency under Ministry of Investment, Trade and Industry (MITI), are thrilled to invite you to the Malaysia-China Halal Business Forum: Malaysia Your Halal Gateway, a premier event designed to foster business connections and explore investment opportunities in the global halal industry.

为了促进商业联系、探索投资机会，作为全球清真产业的重要活动，马来西亚投资、贸易和工业部(MITI)下辖马来西亚清真发展有限公司（HDC）非常荣幸地邀请您参加“马来西亚-中国清真产业论坛：马来西亚--您的清真产业门户”。

Date & Venue|时间地点

Date: 10 September 2024

Time: 9:30 AM – 5:30 PM

Venue: The Peninsula Hotel, Shanghai, China

日期:2024年9月10日

时间:上午9:30至下午5:30

地点:中国 ·上海 · 半岛酒店

Event Highlights|活动亮点

- CEO Breakfast Business Networking Session

- Panel Discussion on Malaysia as the Global Halal Gateway

- VIP Keynote Speeches and Special Remarks

- Halal Business Expansion Opportunity Showcase

- Exclusive Roundtable Dialogue with Minister and Industry Leaders (only 15 seats available)

- CEO早餐商务交流会议

- 嘉宾分享：聚焦马来西亚作为全球清真门户

- 贵宾主旨演讲及特别致辞

- 清真业务拓展机会展示

- 与部长和行业领袖的独家圆桌对话（仅有15个席位）

This is a unique opportunity to engage directly with key stakeholders, explore collaborations, and discover the extensive incentives offered to investors in Malaysia’s thriving halal sector.

本次活动独特高效，可以让您直接接触行业大咖、探索合作可能，并且进一步了解马来西亚为促进清真产业繁荣而提供的多种奖励与优惠。
